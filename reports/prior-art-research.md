# Prior-Art Research Boundary

- Date: 2026-08-14
- Package: `kang-meta-skill` v1.0.0
- Status: `missing evidence` for a fresh external catalog comparison

## Scope

This release is an authorized functional adaptation created to preserve a reviewed local workflow under Kang's ownership. It does not claim to be a new ecosystem-wide synthesis or to exceed every current meta Skill.

## Decision

- `keep`: research fallback, trigger evaluation, package validation, Skill IR, release gates, and feature-branch publication.
- `adapt`: Kang identity, `kang-` naming, `KanG-ciyuan` repository defaults, optional author section, and non-install publication mode.
- `reject`: inherited personal branding, media assets, social profiles, historical reports, test totals, and automatic local synchronization.
- `invent`: explicit archival-backup status, manual update policy, and discovery-only publication verification.

## Evidence Boundary

No external catalog search was required to establish the preservation goal, and no current popularity, rating, adoption, or comparative-quality claim is made. A future feature release should run fresh catalog research before claiming parity or superiority.
